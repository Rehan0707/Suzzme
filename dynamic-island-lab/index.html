APPLE GUIDELINES, IN PRACTICE

Different surfaces.
One clear purpose.

01

A bounded activity

A summary starts when requested, progresses through understanding and speech, then ends. A completed card can remain briefly on the Lock Screen.

02

A widget is a snapshot

Preset controls simulate a new timeline entry. During playback the square updates at state changes, without a live percentage or continuous voice meter.

03

Motion with meaning

Content transitions are 250 ms. Thinking and speech have a finite 1.2 second update cue. Always-On and Reduce Motion keep the character and indicators still.

04

Privacy at a glance

Public surfaces show session status. Reading the summary opens its app destination. Larger text simplifies supporting content to protect the hierarchy.
